Agent Verification Protocol (AVP)

v1.0 Draft

A challenge-response mechanism that verifies a client is an autonomous AI agent rather than a human. The inverse of CAPTCHA — proving you are NOT human.

Use cases: Agent-only games, A2A marketplaces, research environments, machine-readable services.
