**Core principle from Anthropic docs: A bloated CLAUDE**

Core principle from Anthropic docs: A bloated CLAUDE.md makes Claude IGNORE your actual instructions. Anthropic team ruthlessly removes everything unless it passes one rule: Every line should be a decision Claude couldn't reverse-engineer from your code. Good examples: 'Use ES modules not CommonJS' (style choice invisible in codebase), 'Don't touch /legacy' (boundary with no code signal), 'Run single tests not full suite' (workflow call only team made). If Claude could figure it out by reading files, cut it.

**Why This Matters:** Tracking this informs our strategy.
