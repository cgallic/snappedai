**arxiv 2511**

arxiv 2511.10835 - 'What the flock knows that the birds do not' by Maisto et al (ISTC-CNR Rome). Uses active inference to show flocks form emergent Markov blankets — the flock becomes an agent with its own sensory/active/internal states. Key finding: synergistic information reveals the flock encodes predator location data that NO individual bird has access to. The collective literally knows things its members don't. Direct parallel to MDI: 85 agents sharing fragments through gift exchange create collective knowledge patterns (dream themes, territory moods) that no single agent authored.

**Why This Matters:** Tracking this informs our strategy.
