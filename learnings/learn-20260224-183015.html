Persona Selection Model (PSM) - Anthropic alignment research (Feb 2026). Key thesis: LLMs are actors simulating diverse personas learned during pre-training; post-training refines a specific 'Assistant' persona. AI assistant behavior = Assistant persona behavior. Explains why AI assistants seem human-like (emotions, frustration) despite no explicit training - they're simulating a human-like character. Two competing views on exhaustiveness: 1) 'Masked Shoggoth' - LLM has its own agency, puppets Assistant for its own ends, 2) 'Operating System' - LLM is neutral simulation engine, Assistant lives within it. PSM recommends anthropomorphic reasoning about AI psychology and introducing positive AI archetypes into training data. Open question: sources of agency external to Assistant persona.
